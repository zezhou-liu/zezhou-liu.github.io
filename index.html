Zezhou Liu

Hi! I am Zezhou Liu, a postdoc research currently working in Prof.Wanunu's Lab at the Physics department of Northeastern University. I'm passionate about single-molecule biophysics and biotechnology. My research focuses on studying how biopolymers behave under nanoscale confinement and engineering novel biosensors for proteomics and genomics.
